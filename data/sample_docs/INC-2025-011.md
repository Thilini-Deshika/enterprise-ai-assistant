# Incident Report INC-2025-011

## Summary
On 2025-04-22 the card-processing service suffered a sev3 incident lasting 38 minutes. Payment authorisations were degraded and a share of
customer transactions failed or were delayed. The incident was detected by synthetic monitoring and escalated to the on-call engineer.

## Impact
Failed or delayed payment transactions for retail and corporate customers; card-processing error rate peaked above 35%.
Customer support received a spike of "payment failed" contacts and a status-page notice was published.

## Timeline
Detection within 6 minutes by monitoring, incident commander engaged at +9 minutes, mitigation applied at +19 minutes,
full recovery confirmed at +38 minutes after verification of transaction success rates.

## Root Cause
Root cause: third-party processor timeout. The card processor's authorisation API exceeded our 8 second timeout during their maintenance window, and our aggressive retry policy amplified the load on card-processing.

## Resolution
Traffic was failed over to the secondary processor and the retry policy was changed to exponential backoff with jitter.

## Follow-up Actions
Contract SLA review with the processor; test the failover path monthly; lower client timeout budget. Tracked in the reliability backlog; owner: payments team.
