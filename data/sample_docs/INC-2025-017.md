# Incident Report INC-2025-017

## Summary
On 2025-06-30 the fraud-engine service suffered a sev3 incident lasting 55 minutes. Payment authorisations were degraded and a share of
customer transactions failed or were delayed. The incident was detected by synthetic monitoring and escalated to the on-call engineer.

## Impact
Failed or delayed payment transactions for retail and corporate customers; fraud-engine error rate peaked above 35%.
Customer support received a spike of "payment failed" contacts and a status-page notice was published.

## Timeline
Detection within 6 minutes by monitoring, incident commander engaged at +9 minutes, mitigation applied at +27 minutes,
full recovery confirmed at +55 minutes after verification of transaction success rates.

## Root Cause
Root cause: fraud rule misconfiguration. A new velocity rule shipped with a threshold of 3 instead of 30, declining legitimate card payments handled by fraud-engine.

## Resolution
The rule was reverted, affected declines were re-processed, and rule changes now require four-eyes approval and a dry-run.

## Follow-up Actions
Dry-run mode for fraud rules; threshold sanity bounds; staged rollout to 5% of traffic. Tracked in the reliability backlog; owner: security team.
