# Incident Report INC-2026-003

## Summary
On 2026-01-14 the ledger-service service suffered a sev2 incident lasting 88 minutes. Payment authorisations were degraded and a share of
customer transactions failed or were delayed. The incident was detected by synthetic monitoring and escalated to the on-call engineer.

## Impact
Failed or delayed payment transactions for retail and corporate customers; ledger-service error rate peaked above 35%.
Customer support received a spike of "payment failed" contacts and a status-page notice was published.

## Timeline
Detection within 6 minutes by monitoring, incident commander engaged at +9 minutes, mitigation applied at +44 minutes,
full recovery confirmed at +88 minutes after verification of transaction success rates.

## Root Cause
Root cause: database connection pool exhaustion on ledger-service. A retry storm after a slow downstream call kept connections open until the pool (max 200) saturated, so new payment requests queued and then timed out.

## Resolution
Pool size was raised temporarily, the retry storm was stopped by enabling the circuit breaker, and services were restarted in sequence.

## Follow-up Actions
Add pool saturation alerting; cap retries per request; load-test pool sizing quarterly. Tracked in the reliability backlog; owner: payments team.
