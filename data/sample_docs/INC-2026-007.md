# Incident Report INC-2026-007

## Summary
On 2026-02-03 the payment-gateway service suffered a sev1 incident lasting 163 minutes. Payment authorisations were degraded and a share of
customer transactions failed or were delayed. The incident was detected by synthetic monitoring and escalated to the on-call engineer.

## Impact
Failed or delayed payment transactions for retail and corporate customers; payment-gateway error rate peaked above 35%.
Customer support received a spike of "payment failed" contacts and a status-page notice was published.

## Timeline
Detection within 6 minutes by monitoring, incident commander engaged at +9 minutes, mitigation applied at +81 minutes,
full recovery confirmed at +163 minutes after verification of transaction success rates.

## Root Cause
Root cause: faulty configuration deployment. A routing change released without canary validation pointed payment-gateway at a deprecated endpoint; the change was not covered by automated config tests.

## Resolution
The change was rolled back within the incident and a canary stage was made mandatory for routing configuration.

## Follow-up Actions
Mandatory canary for config; schema-validate routing files in CI; one-click rollback. Tracked in the reliability backlog; owner: payments team.
