# Incident Report INC-2026-012

## Summary
On 2026-02-27 the mobile-banking-api service suffered a sev3 incident lasting 52 minutes. Payment authorisations were degraded and a share of
customer transactions failed or were delayed. The incident was detected by synthetic monitoring and escalated to the on-call engineer.

## Impact
Failed or delayed payment transactions for retail and corporate customers; mobile-banking-api error rate peaked above 35%.
Customer support received a spike of "payment failed" contacts and a status-page notice was published.

## Timeline
Detection within 6 minutes by monitoring, incident commander engaged at +9 minutes, mitigation applied at +26 minutes,
full recovery confirmed at +52 minutes after verification of transaction success rates.

## Root Cause
Root cause: message queue consumer lag in the event platform. A partition rebalance combined with an undersized consumer group delayed settlement events for mobile-banking-api by more than an hour.

## Resolution
Consumers were scaled out, the lagging partitions drained, and a lag alert (>5 min) now pages the on-call.

## Follow-up Actions
Autoscale consumer groups on lag; right-size partitions; add a replay tool for settlement events. Tracked in the reliability backlog; owner: platform-engineering team.
