# Incident Report INC-2026-031

## Summary
On 2026-06-21 the payment-gateway service suffered a sev1 incident lasting 187 minutes. Payment authorisations were degraded and a share of
customer transactions failed or were delayed. The incident was detected by synthetic monitoring and escalated to the on-call engineer.

## Impact
Failed or delayed payment transactions for retail and corporate customers; payment-gateway error rate peaked above 35%.
Customer support received a spike of "payment failed" contacts and a status-page notice was published.

## Timeline
Detection within 6 minutes by monitoring, incident commander engaged at +9 minutes, mitigation applied at +93 minutes,
full recovery confirmed at +187 minutes after verification of transaction success rates.

## Root Cause
Root cause: expired TLS certificate on the payment-gateway upstream endpoint. The certificate renewal job had failed silently two weeks earlier and no expiry alert fired, so mutual-TLS handshakes began failing at midnight.

## Resolution
The certificate was renewed manually and the handshake restored; renewal alerting was added with a 21-day warning.

## Follow-up Actions
Automate certificate inventory; alert at 21/7/1 days; add synthetic handshake checks. Tracked in the reliability backlog; owner: payments team.
